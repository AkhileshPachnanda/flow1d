When to Start

Two moments that call for a review.

You're entering a new jurisdiction

New office, new hires, new customers or a new licence. Find out what each state requires before you start operating there.

The rules have changed

A state introduces or amends a requirement that may affect you. We work out the impact, who owns it and what has to happen by when.

How It Works

From instruction to compliance under control.

One central workflow, with each state and business unit completing its own requirements. You can see every step, owner and deadline in your secure Calder & Vance portal.

  1. 01

    Intake & conflict check

    Your General Counsel tells us what's needed, where and how urgently. Our Regulatory Partner confirms we're free to act.

  2. 02

    Scope & engagement

    A Senior Partner proposes scope and fees, you sign the engagement letter, and the matter is opened.

  3. 03

    Regulatory analysis

    We identify the regulations that apply in each jurisdiction and category, bringing in local counsel where a state needs it.

  4. 04

    Obligations & evidence

    We build your obligations register, agree it with you, assign an owner to each item and collect the evidence.

  5. 05

    Compliance review

    Our practice team works through the compliance checklist, and the Regulatory Partner writes the advice memo.

  6. 06

    Gap remediation

    Any gaps are logged with a risk rating. Your teams fix them and we confirm each one is resolved, or escalate it.

  7. 07

    Filings & authorisation

    Local counsel prepares the regulatory filings, you approve them, and we submit them and handle any regulator questions.

  8. 08

    Closing & reporting

    We record the licences and approvals granted, deliver your compliance report and close the matter.

Who's Involved

Your teams and ours, in one place.

Our regulatory team

A Regulatory Partner, a Senior Partner, associates and paralegals run the analysis, review and reporting.

Correspondent counsel

Every request, document, approval and message sits in one shared record, so you always know who owes what, and so do we.
